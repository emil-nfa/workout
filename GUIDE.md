# Workout: install and use

App: **https://emil-nfa.github.io/workout/** · All exercise visuals: **https://emil-nfa.github.io/workout/bakeoff.html**

## Install on the iPhone (2 minutes, once)

1. Open the link above in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share** (the square with an arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open the app **from the new icon**. The Safari tab keeps separate data, so always log from the icon.
4. Go through the 4 setup screens.
5. Open it once with signal. After that it works with no connection (the gym is underground).

## At the gym

- **Today → Start.** The app picks the next letter in the rotation (A, B, C, D; E is an optional bonus). Missed a day? Just do the next letter.
- **Warm-up** checklist at the top, then the exercises in order. Tap an exercise's picture to see the movement, the machine and your own photo of it.
- **Log a set: tap ✓.** Rows are pre-filled with today's suggestion (dim, dotted underline). Did something else? Tap the number and change it with the keypad. The first time on an exercise it asks for the weight: pick one you could do about 3 more reps with.
- **Rest** starts by itself in the bar at the bottom: −15 / +15 change only this rest, Skip ends it. It beeps at the end while the app is open (keep the screen on: Me, Display).
- **The blue chip** tells you what to do: "Add 2.5 kg", "Beat 10 reps", "Drop to 35 kg".
- **Machine taken?** ⋮ → Swap exercise. The app remembers the swap for next time.
- **Superset** (A1/A2): do one set of each back to back, then rest.
- **Finish workout** at the bottom. If some sets aren't ticked, you choose: keep only the ticked ones, or confirm you did them all.
- Leaving the screen is fine: the workout shrinks to a bar above the tabs.

## At home: stretching

**Today → Stretch → Start** (Morning 4 min, Evening 18 min, 5-min fallback). Put the phone on the floor: it speaks the cues and counts down. Tap anywhere to pause; the edges are Back and Skip. Never miss two evenings in a row.

## Keep your data safe

Everything is stored **only on the phone**. **Me → Back up now** about once a week and save the file to Files or iCloud Drive. Deleting the app from the Home Screen deletes its data. To see your log on the Mac: Me → Obsidian notes (or open the app on the Mac and restore a backup).

## Known limits

- No phone ↔ Mac sync (manual backups only for now).
- No alert when a rest ends with the phone locked (a web app can't). The timer is still right when you unlock.
- No vibration on iPhone web apps, and no Apple Health export.
- Machine names are the likely Technogym / Hammer models; exact models weren't confirmed. Photograph the name plates in the app.
- General fitness information, not medical advice. Stop for sharp pain, numbness or tingling.
